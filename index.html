flagshipFastAPIReact PWAClaude visionPlaywrightRailwayAug 2026

Basement Declutter

Take a photo of something in the basement and get a verdict: sell, give away or throw away, with a suggested price. A background pipeline identifies the item with Claude vision, searches Kleinanzeigen for comparable listings, and calculates a condition-aware median price. It then drafts the listing title and description for you.

  • 150+ commits in ten days, planned as epics and tasks with the beads issue tracker
  • Google sign-in with an email allowlist, signed sessions, and an inventory with status tracking
  • Playwright E2E suite runs against the real deployed app on Railway, packaged as its own Docker image
privateClaude CodeGitHub ActionsSep 2026

llm-wiki

A personal knowledge base built on Andrej Karpathy's "LLM Wiki" pattern. An agent ingests raw sources into an interlinked markdown wiki instead of re-searching documents with RAG on every question.

  • Open an issue labelled ingest and Claude opens a PR with the new wiki pages
  • Built on Munich open data: the rent index, Kitas, playgrounds, MVV lines, public toilets, development plans
  • Lint passes on demand catch broken backlinks and contradictions
